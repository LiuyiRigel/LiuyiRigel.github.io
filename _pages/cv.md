Research Summary
======

Undergraduate researcher focusing on industrial artificial intelligence, intelligent inspection systems, computer vision, acoustic emission signal analysis, and deep learning-based fault diagnosis. Current research interests include YOLO-based visual detection, embedded AI deployment, industrial IoT systems, and non-stationary signal analysis for equipment condition monitoring.
